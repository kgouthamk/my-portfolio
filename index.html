From Chat Box to
Omnichannel Intelligence

The story of how I took League's healthcare chatbot and turned it into something that actually works across every channel — not just another chat widget.

Pioneering Agent-Generated UI
League Health · 2025 AG-UI MCP Omnichannel

The problem 🔍: Healthcare AI was stuck in a text box. We were building an AI Health Coach at League — but here's the thing: a plain chat interface creates friction at the exact moment users need clarity. Care plans as walls of text? Medication schedules in paragraph form? That's not just a bad UX — in healthcare, it leads to worse outcomes. We needed the agent to render care plans as structured cards, medication schedules as visual timelines, and health metrics as clear summaries. But building bespoke UI for every surface and content type? That doesn't scale.

The approach 🧩: I pushed hard for Agent-Generated UI — one agent that picks the right content format depending on where the user is, without sacrificing accuracy. We layered open protocols like AG-UI on top of our proprietary design system. The hard part wasn’t the concept — it was proving accuracy at scale. An AI-populated UI component with wrong data isn’t a UX problem — it’s a trust failure. The solution: a single agent that selects the right content representation for each surface, with deterministic data hydration validated through evals and hooks. Visual richness without the hallucination risk.

“Instead of debating architecture in meetings for weeks, I just built the thing ⚡ — wiring up open agent-UI protocols with our design system. Showing beats telling, every time.”

The execution 🏗️: Building the prototype was equal parts engineering and cross-functional leadership. I worked closely with product, engineering, and design partners — and instead of a slide deck, we aligned everyone around a live demo. The prototype used CopilotKit and AG-UI to render the same content as a rich card in-app, structured HTML in email, concise text in SMS, and audio narration for voice. Same agent, different surfaces — adapting intelligently everywhere. And evals made sure every piece of rendered content matched verified source data. Nothing inferred, nothing fabricated.

The surfaces the agent now supports:

In-App (UI SDK) SMS / MMS WhatsApp / Messenger Chat Apps (Claude, Gemini, ChatGPT) Voice / VoIP Email (HTML) Push Notifications

The outcome 🎯: When we demoed the prototype, the team’s reaction was that it felt magical — not because of visual polish, but because it actually did what the theory promised. The same intelligence, adapting seamlessly to every surface and context. What started as a quick prototype became a foundational platform architecture, turning the agent from a chat interface into a true omnichannel intelligent partner — meeting users wherever they already are and speaking the language of that surface ✨
